FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

Ask a question
How long does it take to build a Shopify store?+

Most projects launch within 2–4 weeks from the brief. The exact timeline depends on scope — a focused redesign or new build with clear requirements moves fast. We share a detailed project timeline before we start, so you always know what to expect.

Do you work with existing stores or only new builds?+

Both. We handle full builds from scratch, redesigns of existing stores, performance optimization, migration from other platforms to Shopify, and ongoing development. If your store needs improvement — we'll find the most efficient path to get there.

What makes MILEDEVS different from other Shopify developers?+

We're a Shopify Select Partner — a status given to a small number of agencies worldwide. More importantly, we focus on business outcomes, not just deliverables. Every decision we make is tied to conversions, average order value, and long-term growth. No templates, no guesswork.

How do you make sure the store actually converts?+

We apply proven CRO principles from day one — clear hierarchy, fast load times, trust signals, and optimized checkout flow. We've built and improved dozens of stores across various niches and know what drives purchasing decisions. Results are measurable, not assumed.

What's included in post-launch support?+

After launch, we stay available for bug fixes, minor updates, and technical questions at no extra charge for an agreed period. Beyond that, we offer ongoing retainer support for stores that need regular updates, A/B testing, new feature development, or performance monitoring.
